How to Use a State Scheme Well

Treat it as a base layer

State schemes pay for fixed treatment packages at empanelled hospitals. They are valuable, but not a replacement for your own health policy.

Enrol before you need it

Get your card made while you are well. Sorting eligibility during an emergency is slow.

Ask the hospital's scheme desk

Empanelled hospitals have an Arogya Mitra or similar desk that checks eligibility and handles the paperwork.
