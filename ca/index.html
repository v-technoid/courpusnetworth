Payroll Contributions

  • CPP (5.95%), up to C$74,600, above C$3,500
  • CPP2 (4%), up to C$85,000, above C$74,600
  • EI (1.63%), up to C$68,900
  • CPP, self-employed (11.9%) (self-employed), up to C$74,600
  • CPP2, self-employed (8%) (self-employed), up to C$85,000

Federal tax plus provincial tax for Ontario (with its surtax and health premium), British Columbia or Alberta. Other provinces: federal figure only. Credits for the basic personal amount, CPP, EI and the Canada employment amount are included; low-income reductions and other credits are not.

Self-Employed and Freelancers

  • You pay both halves of CPP (11.9% up to $74,600, then 8% CPP2 up to $85,000).
  • Quarterly instalments are due if your net tax owing is over $3,000.
  • Self-employed people file by 15 June, but any balance is still due on 30 April.
  • GST/HST registration is required once revenue passes $30,000 in four consecutive quarters.

On C$70,000 of profit, tax and contributions come to about C$18,298, so setting aside C$1,525 a month keeps you covered.

Key Dates

  • 15 March: First tax instalment
  • 30 April: File and pay your return
  • 15 June: Self-employed filing deadline, and second instalment
  • 15 September: Third tax instalment
  • 15 December: Fourth tax instalment

Common Questions

How much tax do I pay on C$70,000 in Canada?

For Tax year 2026, about C$16,214 in income tax and payroll contributions, an effective rate of 23.2%, leaving roughly C$53,786 a year. Federal tax plus provincial tax for Ontario (with its surtax and health premium), British Columbia or Alberta. Other provinces: federal figure only.

What are the Tax year 2026 tax rates?

Rates run from 14% to 33%. The full table is above, and the calculator works out your own figure.

Can I track RRSP and other RRSP & TFSA accounts?

Yes. Add them under RRSP & TFSA on the Assets page with your balance and monthly contribution, and they count towards your net worth and retirement projections. 2026 limits: TFSA $7,000, RRSP 18% of last year's earned income up to $33,810.

Is my financial data uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is stored only in your own browser on your device. There is no server, account or tracking, and imported documents are read in the page and never kept.
