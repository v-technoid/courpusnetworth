Grants and Bursaries

Canada Student Grants

College and university

Money you do not repay, for full time and part time students with financial need, students with disabilities and students with dependants. Full time students can get up to $4,200 a year until the end of the 2026 to 2027 school year.

Official site

Canada Learning Bond

Before college

$500 in the first year and up to $2,000 in total, paid into an RESP for children from low income families, with no contribution needed.

Official site

Post Secondary Student Support Program

First Nations and Inuit

Funding for tuition, travel and living costs for eligible First Nations and Inuit students.

Official site

Loans

Canada Student Loans

College and university

Interest on Canada Student Loans was permanently eliminated from April 2023. Repayment starts after you finish studying, and the Repayment Assistance Plan lowers or pauses payments if your income is low.

Official site

Saving for Study

RESP and Canada Education Savings Grant

Before college

Save in an RESP and the government adds 20% of the first $2,500 each year, up to $500 a year and $7,200 per child. Lower and middle income families get up to $100 or $50 more a year.

Official site
