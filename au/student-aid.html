Student Aid and Scholarships in Australia

Grants, loans, savings schemes and scholarship searches for students in Australia, from official sources.

Most domestic students study in a Commonwealth supported place and defer their share of fees with HECS-HELP.

Grants and Bursaries

Youth Allowance for Students

School leavers to 24

Fortnightly payment for full time students aged 16 to 24, means tested on you and often your parents.

Official site

Austudy and ABSTUDY

25 and over, and First Nations students

Austudy supports full time students aged 25 and over; ABSTUDY supports Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander students and apprentices.

Official site

Commonwealth Prac Payment

Teaching, nursing, midwifery, social work

$331.65 a week during mandatory placements for eligible students in these degrees and the Diploma of Nursing, if you get income support or pass a work and income test.

Official site

Free TAFE

Vocational

Tuition free places in priority vocational courses, now permanent under the Free TAFE Act 2025, with at least 100,000 places a year from 2027.

Official site

Loans

HECS-HELP

University

A government loan for your share of fees in a Commonwealth supported place. You repay through tax only once your income passes the repayment threshold ($67,000 in 2025-26), and the balance is indexed each June to the cost of living, not charged interest.

Official site

FEE-HELP and VET Student Loans

University and VET

Loans for full fee places and approved vocational diplomas, repaid the same way as HECS-HELP.

Official site
