Government Schemes and Benefits in Singapore

Savings incentives, family payments, pensions and home buying help in Singapore, each with a link to the official page.

Savings and Retirement

CPF

Mandatory savings for citizens and PRs, split into Ordinary, Special or Retirement, and MediSave accounts, with guaranteed interest floors.

Official site

CPF LIFE

A national annuity that pays a monthly income for life from 65, funded from your Retirement Account.

Official site

Retirement Sum Topping-Up

Top up your own or family members' CPF retirement savings, with tax relief of up to $8,000 for yourself and $8,000 for family each year.

Official site

Matched Retirement Savings Scheme

For citizens aged 55 and above with retirement savings below the Basic Retirement Sum and modest income, the government matches cash top ups dollar for dollar, up to $2,000 a year and $20,000 in a lifetime.

Official site

Supplementary Retirement Scheme

Voluntary savings with tax relief: up to $15,300 a year for citizens and PRs and $35,700 for foreigners.

Official site

Singapore Savings Bonds

Government bonds you can redeem any month without penalty, with interest that steps up the longer you hold.

Official site

Family and Income Support

Baby Bonus and the SG Child Support Package

Today the Baby Bonus gives a cash gift of $11,000 to $13,000 per Singapore citizen child plus a Child Development Account with a First Step Grant and dollar for dollar matching. From April 2027 it is replaced by the SG Child Support Package: for children born from 1 April 2027, a $10,000 Baby Gift, $2,000 Child Credits a year from age 1 to 16, CDA grants and a $10,000 PSEA top up at 17. Children already in the Baby Bonus move across automatically.

Official site

Workfare Income Supplement

Cash and CPF top ups for Singapore citizen workers aged 30 and above earning $500 to $3,000 a month.

Official site

SkillsFuture Credit

Credit for approved courses from age 25, with an extra top up for those aged 40 and above.

Official site

GST Voucher

Cash, MediSave and U-Save utility rebates for lower income citizens and households.

Official site

Silver Support Scheme

Quarterly cash for seniors aged 65 and above who had low incomes over their working life.

Official site

Buying a Home

CPF Housing Grants

First timer families with household income up to $9,000 a month can get the Enhanced CPF Housing Grant of up to $120,000 for a new or resale HDB flat, plus Family and Proximity grants for resale flats.

Official site

Using CPF for a Home

Ordinary Account savings can pay the down payment and monthly instalments, within limits.

Official site
