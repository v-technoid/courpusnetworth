What is CO₹PU$?

A free, private net worth tracker and money planner for India and seven more countries. Add or import what you own and owe, and get one number, a health score and tax calculators.

Read the full answer

Which countries does it support?

India, the USA, the UK, Canada, Australia, Singapore, the UAE and New Zealand, each with its own currency, tax rules, accounts and deadlines.

Read the full answer

Where is my data stored?

Only in your own browser, on your device. There is no server. Move between devices with a backup file from Settings.

Read the full answer

Is my payslip or bank statement uploaded anywhere?

No. Files are read inside the browser tab and let go. Only the numbers you approve are kept, never PAN, UAN or account numbers.

Read the full answer

Do you track me or count visitors?

Only visit counts, with Cloudflare Web Analytics: no cookies, no profile, and it never sees your numbers.

Read the full answer

Does CO₹PU$ use AI or send my data to an AI service?

No. Insights come from readable rules, and photos are read by a text recogniser that runs offline in your browser.

Read the full answer

Which documents can I import?

Payslips, Form 16, bank statements, invoice lists, Form 26AS or AIS, broker holdings, mutual fund CAS and EPF passbooks, as PDF, Excel, CSV or a photo.

Read the full answer

How is net worth calculated?

Everything you own minus everything you owe. ₹68 lakh of assets with a ₹21 lakh loan is a net worth of ₹47 lakh.

Does it work for freelancers and the self employed outside India?

Yes. Pick your country and the freelancer tax calculator uses its rules, from US self employment tax to UK Class 4 NI and Canadian CPP.

Is this financial advice?

No. Its numbers are estimates, not a confirmation. Check big decisions with a qualified professional where you live; our disclaimer lists who, country by country.
