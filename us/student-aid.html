Student Aid and Scholarships in the United States

Grants, loans, savings schemes and scholarship searches for students in the USA, from official sources.

Start with the FAFSA: it is the single form for federal grants, work study and loans, and most states and colleges use it too.

Start Here

FAFSA

College

The Free Application for Federal Student Aid. The form for each school year opens in the autumn before it starts. Fill it in every year you study, as early as you can, because some state and college aid runs out.

Official site

Grants and Bursaries

Federal Pell Grant

Undergraduate

Money you do not repay, for undergraduates with financial need. The maximum award for 2026-27 is $7,395.

Official site

Workforce Pell

Short courses

From 1 July 2026, Pell Grants can pay for approved short job training programs, some as short as 8 weeks, in fields your state says are in demand.

Official site

TEACH Grant

Future teachers

Up to about $4,000 a year (slightly less after automatic federal budget cuts) if you agree to teach a high need subject full time in a school serving low income students for four years within eight years of finishing. If you do not, it turns into a loan.

Official site

State Grants

College

Most states have their own grant programs, often using the FAFSA. Your state higher education agency lists them.

Official site

Loans

Federal Student Loans

College

Direct Loans have fixed rates and income linked repayment. For loans from 1 July 2026, Grad PLUS closes to new borrowers; graduate students can borrow up to $20,500 a year ($100,000 in total), professional students $50,000 ($200,000), and Parent PLUS is capped at $20,000 a year and $65,000 per student. Undergraduate limits did not change.

Official site

Work While You Study

Federal Work Study

College

Part time jobs for students with financial need, often on campus.

Official site

Saving for Study

529 Education Savings Plans

School to college

State run investment accounts where growth is tax free if spent on qualified education, including some K to 12 tuition.
