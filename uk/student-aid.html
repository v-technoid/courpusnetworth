Student Aid and Scholarships in the United Kingdom

Grants, loans, savings schemes and scholarship searches for students in the UK, from official sources.

Student finance depends on where in the UK you live. The links below are for England unless they say otherwise.

Grants and Bursaries

Disabled Students' Allowance

Higher education

Extra support for study costs caused by a disability, long term health or mental health condition, or learning difference. It does not have to be repaid.

Official site

16 to 19 Bursary Fund

Sixth form and college

Help with costs such as travel and books for students aged 16 to 19 in England, from your school or college.

Official site

Student Awards Agency Scotland

Scotland

Scottish students studying in Scotland usually pay no tuition fees, and can get bursaries and loans for living costs.

Official site

Student Finance Wales

Wales

Welsh students can get a mix of grant and loan for living costs, with a larger grant for lower household incomes.

Official site

Loans

Tuition Fee Loan

Undergraduate

Covers your course fees, paid straight to the university. You repay through payroll only once you earn above the threshold, and anything left is written off after 40 years on Plan 5.

Official site

Maintenance Loan

Undergraduate

Help with living costs. How much depends on household income and where you live while studying.

Official site

Student Finance NI

Northern Ireland

Loans and grants for students from Northern Ireland.

Official site
