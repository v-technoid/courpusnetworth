Government Schemes and Benefits in New Zealand

Savings incentives, family payments, pensions and home buying help in New Zealand, each with a link to the official page.

Savings and Retirement

KiwiSaver

Workplace retirement savings. The default rate rose to 3.5% for employees and employers on 1 April 2026 and rises to 4% on 1 April 2028. The government adds 25 cents for each dollar you contribute, up to $260.72 a year, unless your taxable income is over $180,000.

Official site

KiwiSaver First Home Withdrawal

After three years in KiwiSaver you can usually withdraw all but $1,000 of your balance to buy a first home, if your fund allows it.

Official site

NZ Superannuation

A universal pension from 65 for people who meet residence rules, not means tested.

Official site

Family and Income Support

Working for Families

Tax credits for families with children, including Family Tax Credit, In-Work Tax Credit and Best Start for babies.

Official site

FamilyBoost

Get back up to 40% of early childhood education fees, up to $1,560 a quarter, if your household income is under about $57,000 a quarter. Claim every quarter.

Official site

Paid Parental Leave

Up to 26 weeks of government funded payments for eligible parents, matching your usual pay up to a weekly maximum.

Official site

Accommodation Supplement

Help with rent, board or home ownership costs for people on lower incomes.

Official site

Buying a Home

First Home Loan

Loans with a deposit from 5% through participating lenders, underwritten by Kāinga Ora, for eligible first home buyers.
