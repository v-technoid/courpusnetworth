Student Aid and Scholarships in New Zealand

Grants, loans, savings schemes and scholarship searches for students in New Zealand, from official sources.

StudyLink handles student loans and allowances; fees free support now applies to the final year of study.

Grants and Bursaries

Student Allowance

Tertiary

A weekly payment for living costs that you do not repay, based on your or your parents' income.

Official site

Fees Free Final Year

Tertiary

Learners who first started tertiary study from 1 January 2025 and finish their final year by the end of 2026 can get up to $12,000 of final year fees back, paid to their student loan first.

Official site

Māori and Pasifika Trades Training

Trades

Free trades training with support and job placement for Māori and Pasifika learners.

Official site

Loans

Student Loan

Tertiary

Covers fees, course related costs and living costs. It is interest free while you live in New Zealand, and repaid through PAYE once you earn above the threshold.
