Payroll Contributions

  • ACC earners' levy (1.75%), up to NZ$156,641
  • KiwiSaver, your 3.5% (saved for you)

KiwiSaver default contributions rose to 3.5% for employees and employers from 1 April 2026. Self-employed ACC levies are set differently by ACC; the earners' levy is used here as an estimate.

Example: a salary of NZ$75,000 a year.

Income taxNZ$14,720

Total NZ$16,033, an effective rate of 21.4%. Take-home about NZ$56,342 a year, or NZ$4,695 a month after savings contributions.

Self-Employed and Freelancers

  • Self-employed income is taxed at the same rates, filed on an IR3 return.
  • Provisional tax applies once your residual income tax is over NZ$5,000.
  • Put aside money for tax and ACC levies as you invoice.
  • GST registration is required once turnover passes NZ$60,000 a year.

On NZ$75,000 of profit, tax and contributions come to about NZ$16,033, so setting aside NZ$1,336 a month keeps you covered.

Common Questions

How much tax do I pay on NZ$75,000 in New Zealand?

For Tax year 2026-27, about NZ$16,033 in income tax and payroll contributions, an effective rate of 21.4%, leaving roughly NZ$56,342 a year. KiwiSaver default contributions rose to 3.5% for employees and employers from 1 April 2026.

What are the Tax year 2026-27 tax rates?

Rates run from 10.5% to 39%. The full table is above, and the calculator works out your own figure.

Can I track KiwiSaver and other KiwiSaver accounts?

Yes. Add them under KiwiSaver on the Assets page with your balance and monthly contribution, and they count towards your net worth and retirement projections. From 1 April 2026 the default is 3.5% from you and 3.5% from your employer.

Is my financial data uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is stored only in your own browser on your device. There is no server, account or tracking, and imported documents are read in the page and never kept.
