Insurance in New Zealand: What You Have and What to Add

Public cover in New Zealand, how much life and income cover fits you, and plain pros and cons of each type of insurance. Private, free calculator.

How Cover Works in New Zealand

Public hospitals are free and ACC covers accidents for everyone, so the big gaps are illness that stops you working and faster private treatment.

What You Already Have

Public Health System

Free public hospital care for eligible residents, and subsidised GP visits and prescriptions.

Official site

ACC

No fault cover for injuries from accidents, for everyone in New Zealand. If an injury stops you working, weekly compensation pays up to 80% of your earnings, usually from day 8 after the injury.

Official site

Health and Disability Support from MSD

Jobseeker Support with a health condition, and Supported Living Payment if you cannot work long term.

Official site
Health cover
Public care covers emergencies and serious illness, but waits for planned surgery can be long. Private health insurance buys faster specialist and surgical care. Because ACC already covers accidents, choose income cover that focuses on illness.

Types of Insurance, Pros and Cons

Private Health Insurance

Pays for private specialists and surgery, avoiding public waiting lists.

Pros

  • Faster treatment for planned surgery
  • Choice of specialist
  • Often offered by employers

Cons

  • Premiums rise with age
  • Pre existing conditions often excluded
  • Does not replace income

Life Insurance

A lump sum to your family if you die.

Pros

  • Clears the mortgage
  • Protects dependants
  • Cheap when young

Cons

  • Premiums usually rise each year on stepped policies
  • No value if you outlive it
  • Needs reviewing as debts change

Income Protection

Monthly income while illness stops you working.

Pros

  • Fills the gap ACC leaves for illness
  • Premiums are generally tax deductible
  • Can pay until 65

Cons

  • Benefit is offset against ACC payments
  • Waiting periods apply
  • Self employed income can be hard to prove

Trauma Insurance

A lump sum on diagnosis of a listed serious illness.

Pros

  • Money for recovery and treatment
  • Paid regardless of work
  • Can be standalone or with life cover

Cons

  • Only listed conditions
  • More expensive than life cover
  • Definitions matter

Mortgage Protection

Covers mortgage repayments if you cannot work.

Pros

  • Keeps the home safe
  • Simple to size
  • Often cheaper than full income cover

Cons

  • Pays only the mortgage
  • Usually short benefit periods
  • Overlaps with income protection
